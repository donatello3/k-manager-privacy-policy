What is K Manager?

K Manager is a password manager. It lets you store your login credentials (title, username, password, notes, category) in a vault on your device, protected by a master password that you choose. All vault data is encrypted on your device before it is ever stored or, optionally, synced. The Service Provider designed the Application so that your master password and the plaintext content of your vault are never transmitted to or stored on the Service Provider's servers.


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application collects the following information:

  • Account email and password: if you choose to create an account (optional), your email address and account password are sent to and processed by Firebase Authentication (Google) to let you sign in and, if you choose, sync your vault across your devices.
  • Encrypted vault data: if you are signed in and enable sync for an entry, that entry (title, username, password, notes, category) is encrypted on your device using a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2, 100,000 iterations) before being uploaded to Cloud Firestore (Google). The Service Provider cannot decrypt this data — only you, using your master password, can.
  • Cryptographic salt and key verifier: a random salt and an encrypted verification token are stored in Firestore to enable secure sign-in and encryption on multiple devices. These do not contain your master password or vault content.
  • Master password: stored only on your device, never transmitted to the Service Provider. It is used locally to derive the database encryption key and, if you enable biometric unlock, is encrypted with a biometric-bound Android Keystore key that never leaves your device.
  • Biometric data: if you enable biometric unlock, your device's biometric sensor (fingerprint/face) is used exclusively by the Android operating system to unlock a local encryption key. The Application never accesses, collects, or transmits raw biometric data — it only receives a success/failure result from the Android Biometric API.

Registration with the Service Provider is not mandatory. Without an account, all your data stays only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You might not be able to use cloud sync features unless you register.

How can you delete your account and your data?

The Application includes a built-in "Delete Account" feature (accessible from the navigation menu), which, after you confirm your master password, permanently and irreversibly:

  • deletes all your encrypted vault entries from Cloud Firestore;
  • deletes your cryptographic metadata (salt, key verifier) from Cloud Firestore;
  • wipes all local data on your device (encrypted database, encryption keys, biometric data);
  • deletes your Firebase Authentication account itself.

This action cannot be undone. If you are unable to use the in-app feature, you can request deletion of your account and data by contacting the Service Provider at donatello3@yandex.ru; such requests will be processed within the timeframe required by applicable law.

How is your information kept secure?

Your vault data is encrypted on your device using a key derived from your master password (PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations) before it is stored locally (SQLCipher-encrypted database) or, if sync is enabled, uploaded to Cloud Firestore. Your master password itself is never transmitted to or stored by the Service Provider in plaintext. If you enable biometric unlock, your master password is additionally encrypted using an Android Keystore key that is bound to your device's biometric sensor and requires biometric authentication to use; this key is automatically invalidated by the operating system if your device's enrolled biometrics change. However, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.
